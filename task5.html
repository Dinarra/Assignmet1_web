Task 5: Midterm Project Concept & Wireframes

Project Topic: Foodie — Food Delivery Service

Description: "Foodie" is an intuitive online food delivery platform designed to connect hungry users with top local restaurants. The platform targets busy students, professionals, and food lovers seeking fast delivery and seamless ordering. The primary goal is to provide a user-friendly interface for browsing restaurant menus, customizing meal options, tracking orders in real-time, and completing secure online payments.

Project Sitemap (Navigation Structure)

  • Home Page (Featured Restaurants, Search Bar, & Daily Deals)
  • Menu Page (Food Categories, Meal Details, & Customization)
  • Cart & Checkout Page (Order Summary, Payment, & Address Input)
  • Order Tracking Page (Live Delivery Map & Courier Status)
  • User Profile Page (Order History, Favorite Dishes, & Settings)
